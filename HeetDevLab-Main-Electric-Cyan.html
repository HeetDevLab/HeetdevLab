HeetDevLab is an independent software brand focused on making digital experiences worth using.

Founded by Heet Shah, the lab brings together Android security applications, practical web utilities and original game projects. Each project starts with a simple question: can this idea become something useful, clear and enjoyable?

From privacy-minded apps to interactive game systems, the goal is to learn through building and keep raising the quality of every release.
